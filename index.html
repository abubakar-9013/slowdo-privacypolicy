Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

SlowDo is a calm game. It doesn't need to know who you are, and it doesn't try to find out.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • We don't collect, sell or share your personal information.
  • Your progress stays on your device.

What stays on your device

To remember where you are, SlowDo keeps your progress (the levels you've passed, your calm minutes, your longest focus and the days you've played) and your settings (sound, music volume and haptics). This is stored on your device. We never receive it.

Motion

A few levels ask you to tilt or hold your phone still. While you play them, SlowDo reads the motion sensors on your device. These readings are used in the moment and are never stored or sent anywhere.

SlowDo doesn't use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or your Apple Account. The app only learns which level packs you own, so it can unlock them.

Children

SlowDo is for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If any of this ever changes, for example if we add anonymous statistics to see which levels are too hard, we'll update this page and the date at the top before it happens.
